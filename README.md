# RecoverIQ

## AI Revenue Recovery Orchestration Engine

RecoverIQ is an AI-powered revenue recovery system that detects
payment revenue at risk, diagnoses payment failures, predicts
recoverability, selects safe recovery interventions, executes
bounded actions, verifies outcomes, and measures recovered revenue.

> AI recommends. Policy authorizes. Tools execute. Evidence verifies.

---

## Problem

A failed payment does not necessarily mean permanently lost revenue.

Different payment failures require different recovery strategies.
Blindly retrying every failed payment can lead to unnecessary
attempts, poor customer experience, and inefficient recovery.

RecoverIQ treats payment recovery as a decision problem:

1. Identify revenue at risk
2. Understand the payment failure
3. Estimate recoverability
4. Select an appropriate intervention
5. Apply safety policies
6. Execute the permitted action
7. Verify the payment outcome
8. Measure recovered revenue
9. Maintain an audit trail

---

## Solution

RecoverIQ combines:

- Machine Learning
- LLM-based reasoning
- Agentic tool calling
- Deterministic policy enforcement
- Payment APIs
- Webhooks
- PostgreSQL
- Revenue analytics

The system intentionally uses AI only where it provides value.

Financial calculations, authorization, limits, stopping conditions,
and payment verification remain deterministic.
